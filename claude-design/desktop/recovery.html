协议事实Protocol facts 恢复完成后客户端：① 本地生成新 device keypair；② 用恢复的 principal signing key 签 cx.device.authorized，scopes 含 recovery_origin=true；③ 拉取所有 E2EE Space 的 MLS state，处理 epoch gap；④ 仍存在 decryption_pending 项会显示在 audit。 After recovery, the client: ① generates a new device keypair locally; ② signs cx.device.authorized with the recovered principal key (scopes include recovery_origin=true); ③ pulls MLS state for every E2EE Space and handles epoch gaps; ④ any remaining decryption_pending shows up in audit.
